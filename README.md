# 🚗 MOTOR-VEHICLE-THEFT-ANALYSIS-IN-NEWZEALAND-2021-2022
## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing motor vehicle theft data in New Zealand for the years 2021–2022 using Power BI. The objective of this analysis is to identify vehicle theft trends, high-risk locations, most stolen vehicle types, theft patterns over time, and other actionable insights through interactive dashboards and visual analytics.

The project includes:
- Data Cleaning & Transformation (Power BI & PowerQuery Editor)
- Data Modeling
- DAX Calculations
- KPI Dashboard Design
- Interactive Data Visualization
- Insight Generation

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall vehicle theft trends over time
- Identify the most stolen vehicle categories
- Detect high-risk regions and locations
- Analyze theft patterns by month and year
- Compare Vehicle Brands and Models Involved in Theft
- Create interactive KPI dashboards for decision-making
- Generate business insights and recommendations

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

- **Power BI**
- **Power Query**
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)**
- **Microsoft Excel**
- **Data Visualization Techniques**

---

## 📂 Dataset Information

The dataset contains Motor vehicle theft records in New Zealand from 2021 to 2022.

### Key Attributes:
- Vehicle Type
- Vehicle Make
- Vehicle Model
- Theft Date
- Region
- Location
- Vehicle Year
- Color
- Theft Count

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preprocessing

The following preprocessing steps were performed:

- Removed missing/null values
- Corrected inconsistent data entries
- Formatted date columns
- Created calculated columns
- Removed duplicate records
- Standardized column names
- Created Month-Year hierarchy for trend analysis

## 📈 Key Insights

- SUVs and passenger vehicles were among the most stolen vehicle categories.
- Certain months showed significantly higher theft activity.
- Urban regions experienced higher theft rates compared to rural regions.
- Luxury vehicle thefts showed noticeable spikes during specific periods.
- Theft trends revealed recurring seasonal patterns.

---

## 🔮 Predictive & Prescriptive Insights

### Predictive Analysis
- Future theft rates may increase during peak seasonal periods.
- High-value vehicles are likely to remain primary theft targets.
- Theft-prone regions may continue experiencing higher risks.

### Prescriptive Analysis
- Increase surveillance in high-risk areas.
- Implement advanced vehicle tracking systems.
- Improve public awareness regarding vehicle security.
- Strengthen law enforcement monitoring during peak theft periods.
